# Brag Plan: Deen Companion Lite

## What is this app?
A modern, minimal Islamic worship companion for prayer times, Quran, hadith, zikr, charity tracking, and finding mosques — year-round, not just Ramadan.

## The angle
App Store launch energy for a real product: clean feature cards, a phone-frame UI showcase, and the app's actual warm teal-and-gold identity. "Lite" positions it as the essential daily worship toolkit — fast, private, installable.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
App icon scales into a phone frame. Title slams: **Deen Companion Lite**. Subline: *Your complete Islamic worship assistant.*

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Prayer Times** — Recreated dashboard card: "Maghrib in 2h 14m", location pill, mini prayer grid with Maghrib highlighted.
- **Quran of the Day** — Arabic ayah with English translation from the real daily-verse pattern.
- **Zikr Counter** — Tasbeeh count animating 0→33 with Arabic phrase; privacy/offline badge.

## Outro / punchline
**Available now.** iOS · Android · Desktop. Tagline: *Privacy-first. Offline-capable.*

## User flow worth showing
1. **Open app** → Dashboard with next prayer countdown (entry)
2. **Tap feature** → Quran verse of the day with Arabic + translation (key action)
3. **Daily worship** → Zikr counter incrementing (result / habit loop)

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: app store launch video
- Interpretation: Feature-card pacing, smooth slides, professional restraint, title-case copy, no jokes — the product sells itself.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 20 seconds

- Body font: Manrope

## Share copy (draft)
Deen Companion Lite is now live — prayer times, Quran, hadith, zikr, and charity tracking in one minimal app.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm corporate bed with light UI accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: Start at 0s, volume 0.35, fade out last 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: 120 BPM preset. Strong cues at 3.02s (hook settle), 9.02s (Quran reveal), 17.02s (outro lock). Beat grid for feature card arrivals: 5.03, 5.53, 6.03.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; hero glow and phone frame shadow breathe with RMS
- SFX posture: sparse; drop on card arrivals, bell on outro
- Audio-coupled moments: hook icon pop, 3 feature cards sequential, zikr count ticks, final CTA bell
- Restraint rule: no dense SFX stacking; music stays primary

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3s
Phone silhouette on warm background. App icon scales in. Title "Deen Companion Lite" + subtitle "Your complete Islamic worship assistant."
Sequential/interaction: icon pop then title slide up
Audio intent: confident open
Audio-coupled idea: drop on icon land
Music: upbeat bed from 0s
Transition mood: smooth slide → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Prayer Times — 4s
Feature label "Prayer Times & Qibla". Recreated card UI: countdown "Maghrib in 2h 14m", city badge, 6-cell prayer grid with Maghrib highlighted gold.
Sequential/interaction: grid cells highlight one by one (Fajr→Isha)
Audio intent: steady feature reveal
Audio-coupled idea: soft click per grid highlight (accent only)
Music: continue bed
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Quran of the Day — 4s
Feature label "Quran & Hadith". Card with Arabic ayah (RTL) + "Indeed, prayer prohibits immorality and wrongdoing" style translation. Surah label.
Sequential/interaction: Arabic fades in, then translation
Audio intent: calm scholarly moment
Audio-coupled idea: drop on translation reveal
Music: beat-locked reveal ~9s
Transition mood: slide → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Zikr Counter — 4s
Feature label "Zikr Counter". Counter ticks 0→33. Arabic "سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ" with transliteration. Progress bar fills.
Sequential/interaction: count-up animation
Audio intent: satisfying completion
Audio-coupled idea: click ticks on count milestones
Music: continue
Transition mood: soft wipe → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 5s
App icon + "Available now". Sub: "iOS · Android · Desktop". Footer: "Privacy-first. Offline-capable."
Sequential/interaction: CTA lines arrive one by one
Audio intent: resolve and land
Audio-coupled idea: impact bell on "Available now"
Music: fade under final 1.5s; beat-locked at 17.02s
Transition mood: hold → end
